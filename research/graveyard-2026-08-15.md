## What this cannot yet tell you

Nothing, yet — and the numbers above should not be read as a result. The oldest verdict is 20 days old, so no horizon clears both the n≥20 floor and the 90-day window floor.


The window floor is the one that binds. Over a few weeks an individual name's idiosyncratic move dwarfs any plausible judgment edge, so a spread computed there measures noise no matter how many names are in it. The sample size is already fine; the clock is what is missing.


The first genuinely readable column will be **90d**, reachable from 2026-10-24 and meaningful once enough verdicts have crossed it.
