## Reading the result

- **The tested window contains no bear market, and cannot be extended to one.**
  This run starts 2014-01-06 — the earliest date SEC XBRL supports a valuation
  band — and still produces its first episode in 2017. Point-in-time
  fundamentals are empty until 2011 (zero fiscal years visible as of Jan 2009
  and Jan 2010), and a band is low-confidence below 7 years, so the funnel is
  structurally blind before ~2017. Every result here describes one regime,
  2017–2026, which had no sustained bear market.
- **The portfolio skew is the dip-buyer's signature.** Mean excess positive at
  every horizon, median negative, hit rates 38–48%: the aggregate is carried by
  a small tail of 2018Q4/COVID winners.
- **The discrimination test is inverted but unusable** — survivorship
  concentrates in the vetoed cohort; needs historical constituents.
- **The ladder entry beats the tranche entry on every equal-weight metric.**
  Same episodes, same funnel: 1y mean excess +7.5% vs +1.8%, and the 1y median
  flips positive (+1.9% vs −1.9%) — the only entry variant in any test to
  manage that. 3y and to-date improve similarly. On the money-weighted curve
  the two are a wash relative to paired VOO (both ~1.14×), because the curve
  is dominated by the same tail winners — the ladder's edge is that the
  *typical* fill stops losing to the index, achieved with ~18% less capital
  deployed (726 vs 885 fills) and one rule instead of three legs. Part of the
  gain is the weekly clean-sweep re-check, which the price-only sweeps could
  not credit: the ladder refuses fills the tranche rules happily made.

## Honest limits

- **No Stage-2 judgment** — the temporary-vs-permanent diagnosis (the claimed edge) cannot be replayed without hindsight; this measures the mechanical funnel alone.
- **Survivorship** — universe is today's index membership; the blowups that left it never enter. Portfolio results are a **ceiling**; the discrimination gap is less affected (both cohorts share the bias) but not immune.
- Config labels (sector/financial/cyclical) are today's; `rejected` ignored (hindsight).
- Price-only returns, both sides. Dividend-heavy value names are understated.
- Early-sim valuation bands are shorter than 10y (prices ~15y, XBRL ~2009+); names enter as their own history reaches the minimum.
- Tranche 2b (stabilization) and T3 (confirmation) are mechanical proxies for judgment rules; episode veto status is classified at open only.
- ADR and class-dimensioned-share names skipped (price-signal-only in the live pipeline). A positive result here does NOT validate the strategy; a negative portfolio result IS meaningful evidence against the mechanical funnel, and the discrimination gap is the highest-powered number in this report.
