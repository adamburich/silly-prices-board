## 2. Architecture conflicts — these need decisions, not code

### 2.1 The reserve is not SPY. It is g67.

**This is the largest gap between the handoff and the running system.**

```
handoff §3.1 :  A reserve = SPY (67% sleeve) + separate protected B (33%)
live config  :  reserve_weights = (("QQQ", 0.667), ("SPY", 0.333))
```

The live reserve *is* the 2/3-QQQ blend that §6.2 identifies as the misleading
Stage-2 hurdle and §8 lists as REJECTED. But in the live system it is not a
benchmark — it is the **actual asset the ladder liquidates to fund fills**.

The two are not the same claim, and the handoff does not distinguish them:

- As a *benchmark*, g67 was wrong because B is separately funded and cancels
  out of the marginal A decision. That reasoning is sound.
- As an *asset*, the QQQ-heavy reserve was a deliberate, documented decision
  (`reports/megacap-growth-reserve-2026-08-11.md`; the config comment records
  that every fraction is equally supported and more growth buys more return and
  more crisis starvation, monotonically).

The migration the handoff implies is real and worth stating plainly: **growth
exposure moves out of the reserve and into a protected B sleeve.** Today QQQ-in-
reserve does double duty — funding buffer *and* growth coverage — and the ladder
can spend it. Under the handoff, SPY is the funding buffer and B is protected
from A entirely. Total growth exposure is similar; what changes is that A can no
longer consume it.

That is an improvement, but it is a migration of live positions, not a rename.

### 2.2 No sleeves exist

There is no A/B sleeve separation in `main`. There is one `AUTO` account with a
blended reserve inside it, plus `MECH` (shadow lane) and three controls
(`CTRL-VOO`, `CTRL-QQQ`, `CTRL-BRK-B`).

Handoff invariants 1-4 (A never sells B; no-A path reproduces SPY; B identical
in treatment and control; no leverage) **cannot currently be tested, because the
structure they constrain does not exist.** Phase 1 has to build it.

Note also that the existing control is `CTRL-VOO`, not SPY, and the config
records a deliberate reason for SPY-not-VOO in the reserve (AUTO holds 33
historical paired-VOO lots; FIFO would sell those first). Any SPY/VOO decision
has to respect that.

### 2.3 Stage-2 is three-valued, the handoff is binary

```
handoff  :  BUY | REJECT
live     :  temporary | permanent | uncertain   (+ pending, error)
```

The mapping is unambiguous and already implemented as a veto:

| live | handoff |
|---|---|
| `temporary` | BUY |
| `permanent`, `uncertain`, `pending`, `error` | REJECT |

This matches §4.3's "default to REJECT when the affirmative case is not strong"
— `uncertain` is exactly that default. Recommend keeping three values internally
(they carry real information the graveyard analysis stratifies on) and treating
BUY/REJECT as a derived projection, not a replacement.

### 2.4 Authorization already expires on a calendar — the handoff does not mention this

§4.3 and invariant 12 say authorization "persists until trim," and §7.6 argues
against rediagnosis at ladder-episode reset. Live behaviour is neither: verdicts
carry `[diagnose] max_age_days = 7` and are re-researched **weekly**, plus event
invalidation (12% price move, new filing, buy-line cross, disqualifier change).
As of 2026-08-14 `permanent` verdicts get a 90-day clock instead.

So the system already performs calendar reauthorization on a different axis than
the one §7.6 studied. The handoff's conclusion (don't rediagnose at *episode
reset*) is not in conflict with it, but the interaction is unstudied and should
be an explicit decision rather than an accident. This is §13 item 11.

**Delivered:** `sillyprices/sleeves.py` + `tests/test_sleeves.py` (14 tests) +
`.github/workflows/invariants.yml`.

Invariants 1-4 are now executable release blockers rather than prose:

| invariant | how it is enforced |
|---|---|
| 1 — an A purchase never sells B | B is reachable only from `contribute`; a short reserve raises `LeverageError` instead of reaching further |
| 2 — no purchases ⇒ A reproduces SPY | asserted against a hand-computed pure-SPY share count over four cashflows at four prices |
| 3 — B identical in treatment and control | two portfolios stepped in parallel, B compared each step, with a guard asserting A actually diverged |
| 4 — no leverage | overdraft rejected, exact drain allowed, reserve asserted non-negative under repeated draws |

`check_invariants()` runs after every mutation, so a later edit that lets A
reach into B fails loudly rather than silently reallocating the portfolio.

**The suite was mutation-tested.** A deliberate bug was introduced — on a short
reserve, quietly liquidate B pro rata to cover the shortfall, which is precisely
what invariant 1 exists to prevent — and three tests failed. A green suite that
has never been shown to fail is not evidence. `InvariantsCanFail` keeps a
permanent version of that check by driving the model into each forbidden state
through a back door.

**Deliberately deferred to Phase 2, not forgotten.** The handoff's Phase 1 also
lists candidate / decision / ladder state and the Stage-2 interface. Those are
not built, because they would be dataclasses with no consumer and no invariant
to satisfy — speculative structure is what the handoff warns against carrying
over from the research fork. They arrive in Phase 2 alongside the code that
uses them. Prices are already injected as a plain mapping, which is the
market-data interface invariants 1-4 actually needed.

**Not wired to the live ladder.** `autotrade.py` still runs the existing
blended-reserve path, and nothing imports `sleeves.py` yet. That separation is
intentional: the live ladder trades, and this model has not earned that yet.
Phase 2 ports A onto it; Phase 4 migrates the reserve.

### §13.2 was not a confirmation — it was a defect

`build_bands` shipped with `max_years = 10`, truncating the percentile base to a
trailing ten fiscal years. Measured 2026-08-14: **104 of 120 sampled names carry
more than ten annual records, most 18-19 years**, so roughly half the visible
history of the typical name was being discarded. That is the literal
trailing-10-year construction handoff §4.2 records as tested and REJECTED, while
the research baseline it was compared against used expanding history.

Fixing it re-rated the board: **63 names changed rating, BUY 21 → 12, RICH +27.**
Directionally expected — restoring the cheaper 2008-2015 era makes today's
multiples look richer against a fuller history.

Six names became NO-VALUE, and that is a genuine second-order effect rather than
a bug: a longer history pulls in crisis years (2007-09 for the financials,
737-MAX/COVID for BA) and trips the EXISTING erraticness guard — "P/B history
too thin or erratic for a reliable normal". Those names lose their band and fall
back to price-signal-only. The guard was not touched; re-tuning it would be
exactly the threshold optimisation §7.2 forbids. Worth a look as its own
question.

### §13.11 changed live behaviour too

A STALE `temporary` verdict previously still authorized new fills — `pending` was
recorded but never consulted. Seven names are now held back by the freshness
gate (AVB, DECK, FRT, LDOS, PNR, STE, TROW): thesis intact, evidence aged, adds
suspended until Stage 2 renews them. That part is self-healing — the daily
routine re-researches the queue.

**Cumulative: the AUTO BUY universe went 21 → 6.** Both reductions are the
instructed semantics, not drift.

### Safety verification (requested 2026-08-14)

Failing closed on Stage 2 blocks ENTRY and must never trap a position. Verified
executably in `tests/test_exit_paths_unblocked.py`:

- `review.sell_signal(h, row)` takes **no config**, so it cannot be gated by one.
- `broker.sell` and the whole of `review.py` are free of any `stage2_*` reference.
- *(Terminology superseded 2026-08-15; the invariant holds.)* With Stage 2 unconfigured, a thesis break still yields SELL-REVIEW and a fired
  disqualifier still yields REVIEW.
- A row the veto rewrote to FLAGGED still classifies for exit — it does not
  become UNTRACKED, which would have blinded the sell side during a fail-closed
  run.
- The veto mutates a `rating` only; it never touches a holding.

The asymmetry is the invariant: **no new exposure, every exit path open** —
manual, administrative, corporate-action, or otherwise independently authorized.
