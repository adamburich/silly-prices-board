## Reading the result

- **The portfolio skew is the dip-buyer's signature.** Mean excess is positive at
  every horizon while the median is negative and hit rates run 39–48%: most
  mechanical buys underperform the index, and the aggregate is carried by a small
  tail of huge winners (2018–2020 cohorts: KLAC, LRCX, FLEX, FIX, EME — semis and
  construction bought in the 2018Q4 and COVID panics). Without those two windows
  the mechanical funnel loses. This is exactly the shape the methodology predicts
  for cheapness-alone buying — the edge, if any, must come from the diagnosis
  stage this backtest cannot replay.
- **The discrimination test came back inverted** (vetoed episodes outperformed
  passed ones), but do not conclude the trap sweep is harmful. The veto census is
  dominated by net-dilution / Altman-Z / sector-wide flags, which fire hardest at
  maximum panic (COVID, energy 2020) — precisely the buy points with the largest
  rebounds *among survivors*. Survivorship bias concentrates in the vetoed
  cohort: the distressed names that actually died left today's index and never
  enter the test. This number cannot be trusted until the universe uses
  historical constituents.

## Honest limits

- **No Stage-2 judgment** — the temporary-vs-permanent diagnosis (the claimed edge) cannot be replayed without hindsight; this measures the mechanical funnel alone.
- **Survivorship** — universe is today's index membership; the blowups that left it never enter. Portfolio results are a **ceiling**, and the bias concentrates in the VETOED cohort (distressed-then-died names are absent), inflating its apparent returns.
- Config labels (sector/financial/cyclical) are today's; `rejected` ignored (hindsight).
- Price-only returns, both sides. Dividend-heavy value names are understated.
- Early-sim valuation bands are shorter than 10y (prices ~15y, XBRL ~2009+); names enter as their own history reaches the minimum.
- Tranche 2b (stabilization) and T3 (confirmation) are mechanical proxies for judgment rules; episode veto status is classified at open only.
- ADR and class-dimensioned-share names skipped (price-signal-only in the live pipeline). A positive result here does NOT validate the strategy; a negative portfolio result IS meaningful evidence against the mechanical funnel, and the discrimination gap is the highest-powered number in this report.
